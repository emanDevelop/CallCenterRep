# Yasmeen Cloud — Brand Guide

> Yasmeen Cloud (سحابة الياسمين, short name "Yasmeen Digital") is a **fictional company**
> used for learning and demos. All facts, prices and contact details are made up.

## Who we are

A digital-solutions company headquartered in Amman, Jordan, with branches in Dubai, Cairo and
Riyadh. We build websites, online stores and mobile apps, and offer a **smart voice assistant**
that answers customer calls 24/7 in Arabic (Levantine, Gulf, Egyptian) and English.

- Arabic name: سحابة الياسمين للحلول الرقمية
- English name: Yasmeen Cloud Digital Solutions
- Tagline (AR): حلول ذكية، خدمة إنسانية
- Tagline (EN): Smart solutions, human service
- Product hero line (AR): المساعد الصوتي الذكي — اسألنا عن خدماتنا وأسعارنا وساعات الدوام، بصوتك.
- Product hero line (EN): Smart Voice Assistant — Ask about our services, prices and opening hours, just by talking.

## Audience

Small and medium businesses across the Arab world that want to go digital without losing the
personal touch: shop owners, clinics, service companies, startups.

## Personality and voice

Warm, calm, modern, trustworthy. Friendly like a helpful neighbour, never pushy.
Arabic copy is primary and written right-to-left; English is secondary.
Keep copy short and clear. Speak in a natural Jordanian tone in Arabic.

## Logo

A five-petal jasmine flower: five rounded petals around a small round centre.
Petals use a diagonal gradient from the primary accent to the secondary accent.
The centre is the background colour (a "hole"), so the logo works on both themes.
See `yasmeen-logo.svg`. Keep clear space of one petal width around it. Never stretch or recolour
outside the palette.

## Typography

- Arabic: **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** (600–700 for headings, 400–500 for body)
- Latin: **Inter** (700–800 for headings, 400–500 for body)
- Big, centred headings with a soft gradient from text colour to the accent.

## Visual motifs

- **Jasmine flowers**: small five-petal flowers floating softly down the background, half
  transparent, in the theme's accent shades (violet/pink at night, teal/navy by day).
- **The voice orb**: a glowing, softly wobbling sphere that reacts to voice — the symbol of the
  assistant listening and speaking. Soft radial glow, glossy highlight top-left.
- **Soft glows**: large blurred radial light in the corners of dark backgrounds.
- Rounded cards (24px radius), glassy surfaces with subtle borders, pill-shaped buttons.

## Imagery direction

- Calm, premium, uncluttered. Lots of negative space.
- Dark scenes lit by violet/pink glow, or bright airy scenes with teal and navy.
- Show the assistant as the glowing orb, phones or laptops with the voice UI, and real-life
  moments of business owners and customers getting quick answers (Arab, modern, diverse).
- Avoid: stock-photo clichés, harsh neon, cluttered collages, robots, cartoon mascots,
  generic "AI brain" circuitry.
